Privacy Policy

This is a student portfolio project — this page exists to demonstrate a complete site structure, not as a binding legal document.

What's stored

If you create an account, your username, email, and a securely hashed password are stored in this project's database. Bookmarks and search history are tied to your account so features like "saved articles" work.

Third-party services

News content is fetched live from NewsAPI. Chatbot responses are generated by Google Gemini. Neither service is controlled by this project — see their own privacy policies for how they handle data.
